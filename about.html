Our Story

We opened Paceline in 2015 with one goal to build the running shop we would always wanted. A decade in, we're still hand picking every shoe, every layer and every accessory for runners just like us.

01 — Why we exist

We are all about the run.

Fashion can be beautiful, timeless, responsible, and human.

if your aim is 5k or 26.2 miles, on road or trail, we're here toprepare, empower and equip you from your first training run through to race day. Our tried and tested range covers road trail track and field apparel and accessories with zero compromise on performance or style.

Pceline is small and independent, but the community around it is anything but. We run our own weekly club, host free gait analysis, and partner with local chariteis so 20% of profits from kids go to Girls Run
